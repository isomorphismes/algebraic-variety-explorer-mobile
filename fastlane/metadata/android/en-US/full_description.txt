Enter a polynomial in x, y, and z and explore its real zero set as a shaded
three-dimensional surface.

Drag to rotate, pinch to zoom, load classic example varieties, and export the
current rendering as a PNG.

Algebraic Variety Explorer is adapted from IMAGINARY's SURFER and uses the
original Apache-licensed jsurf ray-tracing core by Christian Stussak.

The app works entirely offline and includes no ads, analytics, accounts, or
network permissions.
